# 12 — ASSET INTELLIGENCE & MARKET PULSE

## Goal
Use Singha transaction/engagement data and approved external sources to create real market intelligence.

## Internal metrics
Possible:
- views;
- watchers;
- bidders;
- bid count/velocity;
- opening/reserve/final;
- sell-through;
- time-to-sell;
- category;
- make/model;
- condition;
- location;
- sale method.

## Comparables
Return:
- comparable asset IDs;
- similarity reasons;
- historical sale values/dates;
- differences;
- confidence.

Do not present an estimate as guaranteed valuation.

## Seller intelligence
AI/analytics may recommend:
- sale mode;
- auction event;
- opening range;
- demand strength;
- comparable range;
- likely buyer interest.

Human approval.

## Market indices
Future:
- used commercial vehicle;
- machinery;
- gem auction;
- regional demand.

Methodology/version must be documented.

## External news pipeline
```text
approved source
 -> ingest original metadata
 -> dedupe/classify
 -> AI summarize
 -> rules/human review
 -> Market Pulse
```

Never publish invented news.

Store source reference, publication/ingest date, model/version and approval state.

## Homepage Market Pulse
Compact editorial section:
- market movement;
- notable Singha result;
- industry/government update;
- upcoming major event;
- demand trend.

Do not turn homepage into a news portal.

## Content engine
Future drafts:
- Morning Market Brief
- Weekly Asset Market Report
- category reports
- seller reports
- personalized buyer brief
